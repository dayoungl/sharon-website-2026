Team plan

Building Runway’s teams business

Role
Design lead
Timeline
3 weeks · September 2026
Scope
Feature gating, invites, upsells and the marketing page

Small business owners were our largest source of recurring non-Enterprise revenue, yet they were paying as individuals, with no plan built for teams.

I owned the plan’s in-product experience: an invite flow that shows seat count and cost before anyone hits send, upsell dialogs that sell the feature someone just tried to use, and gating that moved collaboration features and admin settings like SSO to the Team plan. I also built the marketing page on our CMS.

Getting there meant aligning Product, Prosumer and Enterprise on an open-ended brief; next, domain routing and discoverable projects will help new members find their team’s workspace.
