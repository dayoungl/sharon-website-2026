Iris Lunar Rover

The world’s first student-led lunar rover

Role
Product Designer, Design Systems
Timeline
Aug 2019 — Jul 2020
Scope
Teleoperation software and its design system

Iris is the world’s first student-led lunar rover, and the smallest and lightest ever built. It launched on a ULA rocket from NASA’s Kennedy Space Center on January 8, 2024, on a mission to look for traces of water on the Moon’s surface, the first phase of NASA’s Artemis mission to return humans to the Moon.

As product designer on the design systems side, I crafted the user experience of the rover’s teleoperation software, which the mission control team on Earth uses to drive Iris remotely.

I established a design system that keeps its four interfaces (image viewer, map, telemetry visualization and command line) consistent, and worked with the science, operations and systems teams to find pain points and define user needs, testing them in a virtual mission environment we prototyped in Unity.
